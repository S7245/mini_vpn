## 2026-08-26 - Deadline evidence belongs at actor admission

- Root scheduling time answers only whether an absolute timer is due. It cannot
  prove when a transport record reached the endpoint. Capture arrival after the
  complete record body is read and inside the same atomic reservation that
  publishes the queue epoch.
- Publication epoch and arrival time are complementary: the epoch closes the
  queue-Empty/in-flight race, while immutable same-clock arrival decides
  before/equal/after deadline eligibility. A blocked retry must move both
  values unchanged and never recapture time.
- Every physical leg in one endpoint session must share one clock source, not
  merely produce numerically comparable timestamps. Reject a different source
  before standby/controller mutation and fail closed on rollback or overflow.
- A first hard-terminal timestamp must survive cleanup overlap without becoming
  cloneable ambient authority. One canonical time plus a fixed pair of private
  non-Clone witnesses lets handoff and root outcome converge at join; checked
  capacity must include the canonical value and both handles.
- Reducer-derived application progress is narrower than decoded ACK or QUIC
  delivery. Emit an observation only after positive committed advancement
  releases nonzero replay bytes; duplicates, rejection, and transport-only ACK
  evidence must not refresh recovery time.
- A value-level rejection/arbiter kernel is a useful deterministic seam, but it
  is not production reachability. Do not accept full R7 until physical B
